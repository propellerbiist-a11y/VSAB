# VSAB
Mission: Carry temperature-sensitive vaccines or blood from a district hospital to a remote health centre. Requirements: 2 kg insulated payload kept at 2-8°C for the whole trip; 25 km one way; wind 10 m/s with gusts to 15 m/s; light rain; BVLOS failsafes; winch drop or precision landing in 5 m x 5 m; takeoff weight under 25 kg.
